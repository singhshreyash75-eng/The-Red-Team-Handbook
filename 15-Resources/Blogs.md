
# Cybersecurity Blogs

> Curated technical blogs for vulnerability research, web exploitation, red teaming, Active Directory, reverse engineering, and offensive-security research.

## 1. Overview

Cybersecurity blogs provide practical knowledge that often goes beyond textbooks and certification courses.

They are useful for:

- Understanding real-world vulnerability discoveries
- Learning exploitation techniques and root causes
- Following new security research
- Studying vulnerability disclosure reports
- Improving penetration-testing methodology
- Understanding modern attack surfaces

A useful learning process:

```text
Research Article
      ↓
Understand Vulnerability
      ↓
Identify Root Cause
      ↓
Reproduce in Authorized Lab
      ↓
Study Mitigations
      ↓
Document Findings
```

## 14. How to Study Research Blogs

Do not simply read an article and move on.

For every interesting vulnerability:

```text
1. Identify affected technology

2. Understand the vulnerability class

3. Determine the root cause

4. Identify required preconditions

5. Study the attack surface

6. Reproduce safely in a lab

7. Understand mitigations

8. Record lessons learned
```

## Key Takeaway

The most valuable security blogs explain:

```text
VULNERABILITY
      ↓
ROOT CAUSE
      ↓
EXPLOITATION MECHANISM
      ↓
SECURITY IMPACT
      ↓
MITIGATION
```

Prioritize technical depth and reproducible research over sensational vulnerability headlines.
